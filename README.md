Hello, to start, I will give you a preview of what the website entails and other details of its build will be explained further in a live session or later in this README.md file.
This website is basically here to ensure more security to students in our universities when they are planning or attending events around campus.
There are many of such events, but what makes this safer is the fact that every event in these campuses is confirmed by the administration and the local authorities have to be informed.
For the event organiser, he/she should verify their identity i.e KYC to avoid scamming.
